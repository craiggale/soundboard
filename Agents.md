# Soundboard

## Overview
Soundboard is a lightweight Next.js web app built for Accenture Song. It replaces a standard Microsoft Form with a simple, engaging experience that encourages people to share how they're using AI tools in their day-to-day work.

## Goal
Gather insights across Song's Creative, Experience, and Customer Technology practices to help inform where Accenture invests in AI tooling going forward. Specifically:
- Which AI tools are people actually using?
- Which do they value most?
- How are they applying them in their work?

## Context
The project is scoped for Song Unplugged — an internal community initiative featuring informal, expert-led sessions on topics like generative AI, loyalty management, employer branding, and sustainability. Soundboard should feel consistent with that spirit: human, conversational, and low-friction.

## Technical
- **Framework:** Next.js
- **Hosting:** Internal Lovelace platform (Accenture)
- **Scope:** Small build — target ~2 hours of development time

## Design Principles
- Engaging and approachable — not another boring form
- Simple and fast to complete
- Creative freedom encouraged
